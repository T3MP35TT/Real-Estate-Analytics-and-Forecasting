# EDA findings guide

Generated from the supplied listing dataset. Read `city_price_summary.csv` for the ranked city medians. The source has no crime-rate or infrastructure-score field and no resale observations, so these analyses are not represented by synthetic data. `Investment_Return_Proxy` is a rule based on assumed appreciation less an age adjustment; it is not observed return. School and hospital density scores are counts per 1,000 sq ft, a simple listing-level proxy rather than geographic density. Review the charts alongside these limitations.
